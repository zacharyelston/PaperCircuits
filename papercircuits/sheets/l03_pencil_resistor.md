# L03  Draw a resistor

A pencil line conducts, a little. Slide a wiper along one to dim an LED.

## You need

- {LED1.refs}: bright red LED, 5 mm, clear lens
- {R1.refs}: {R1} ({R1.bands}) {P}
- A soft 6B pencil and an eraser
- 5 mm copper tape and clear tape
- Scissors
- Power page P1; a multimeter

## Build it

1. Shade track A and track B with the 6B pencil. Press hard and go over each one at least 10 times,
   until it shines. Stay inside the lines.
2. Rub out any pencil in the gap between track A and the V+ lane. A smudge there joins them.
3. {lanes_step}
4. {hole_step}
5. Lay R1's top lead flat across the end of track A and hold it down with clear tape. Its bottom
   lead goes on the lane below. Either way round works. {T1}
6. Place LED1 as printed: long leg on the +.
7. Hold R1's bottom lead and both LED1 leads flat with a patch of copper tape. {T0}
8. {wiper_step}
9. Bind this page with P1 and switch BT1 on. Press the wiper across the V+ lane and track A, then
   slide it.
