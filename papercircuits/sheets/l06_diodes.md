# L06  Diodes are one-way valves

A diode lets current through one way only. Which LED gets any?

## You need

- {LED1.count} red LEDs, 5 mm
- {R1.count} resistors, {R1} ({R1.bands}) {P}
- {D1.count} small diodes, {D1} (glass, black band)
- 5 mm copper tape
- Power page P1 in the binder; a multimeter

## Build it

1. {lanes_step}
2. {flap_step}
3. {hole_step}
4. Place R1 and R2 across their gaps, either way round.
5. Place D1 and D2 **exactly as printed**, black band on the band mark. D2 points the other way on
   purpose.

## Labels

- switch: fold the corner down
- forward: D1 forward
- reversed: D2 reversed

## Predict

- prompt: D2 is printed the other way round.
- question: When you fold the corner down, which LEDs will light? Circle one for each.
- choices: lights  /  stays dark
- why: Why do you think so?

## Measure

- prompt: Hold the corner down. Black probe on TP6.
- table: Diode
- D1: D1  TP2, TP3
- D2: D2  TP4, TP5
- before: Before
- after: After
- across: Across
- rule: Across = before − after

## Explain

- prompt: D1 takes about 0.7 V. Where does the rest of the supply go?
- question: Why is LED2 dark, and why is there so much voltage across D2?
