# M7  Output

Mix up to three signals and send them to a real amp.

## You need

- Bare wire for {loops} patch loops
- A 3.5 mm plug-to-clip lead
- A powered speaker or amp with a line input
- Copper tape, solder, 2-core cable

## Caption

Patch an oscillator's OUT to IN1. Clip OUT and GND to the 3.5 mm plug, then plug into the amp.

## Before you plug in

Only a powered speaker's or an amp's line (AUX) input.

Turn its volume right down first, then bring it up slowly.

Never headphones, and never a bare speaker.

## Build it

1. Lay the power ring and the {lanes} lanes inside it, one piece of tape each.
2. Place R1 to R3 from each IN lane to the MIX lane, C1 from MIX to OUT, and R4 from OUT down to
   the GND rail. All go either way round.
3. {lead_step}
4. Solder a patch loop upright on each loop mark: IN1 to IN3, OUT and GND. {T0}
5. {edge_join}

## Play it

- Turn the amp's volume right down. Clip OUT to the plug's tip and ring, and GND to its sleeve.
  Plug into the amp's line or AUX input.
- Patch M1's OUT to IN1 and play a key, then bring the volume up slowly.
- A second oscillator on IN2 plays at the same time: the two notes mix.

## Schematic note

OUT and GND go to the plug: tip and ring to OUT.
