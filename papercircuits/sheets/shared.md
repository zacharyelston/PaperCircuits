# Shared words

Snippets any page's words can use by name, in braces. Tags and values work here too.

## lead_step

Solder every lead to its lane, or hold it flat with a small patch of copper tape. {T0}

## hold_step

Hold each lead flat on its lane with a small patch of copper tape. {T0}

## hole_step

Punch the holes and set eyelets in H2 and H3. {T0}

## lanes_step

Lay the {lanes} lanes, one piece of tape each. Fold at the creases; never cut.

## flap_step

Stick a {flap_pad} mm piece of copper tape on the corner flap, S1. {T0}

## bind_step

Bind this page with P1, then fold the corner down to switch on.

## crossing_step

Where two lanes cross, lay the lower one, stick the insulating patch over it, then lay the upper one.

## wiper_step

Cut out the wiper. Stick {wiper_strip} mm of tape on it where marked, then turn it copper side down.

## edge_join

Join the edge connectors to the pages either side with 2-core cable, or with red and black clip leads.
