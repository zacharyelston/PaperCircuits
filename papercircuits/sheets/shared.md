# Shared words

Snippets any page's words can use by name, in braces. Tags work here too.

## lead_step

Solder every lead to its lane, or hold it flat with a small patch of copper tape. {T0}

## edge_join

Join the edge connectors to the pages either side with 2-core cable, or with red and black clip leads.

## hole_step

Punch the holes and set eyelets in H2 and H3. {T0}
