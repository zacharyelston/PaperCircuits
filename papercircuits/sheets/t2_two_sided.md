## You need

- Four cards printed double-sided (pages 1 and 2)
- 5 mm and 12 mm copper tape, a craft knife and a pin
- Cut-off resistor legs and solder; small eyelets, about 3 mm
- A binder clip, a sheet of plain paper and one of vellum
- A multimeter with a 200 Ω range

## 1 · Registration

1. Check the 100 mm bar on both sides of one card.
2. Push a pin straight through the centre of each front mark, R1 to R4.
3. On the back, read where each hole landed on the 1 mm scale, across and up or down.

Pass: within 1.5 mm everywhere. Lanes overlap {via_overlap} mm at wire and eyelet vias.

## Registration table

- Across
- Up / down

## 2 · Vias

1. **V1:** cut the two slits. Lay one piece of tape from A, through the first slit, along the back and
   up through the second slit, to B.
2. **V2:** lay the three pieces. Push a resistor leg through each via mark, bend it flat on both sides
   and solder it.
3. **V3:** lay the three pieces, pierce a small hole through both at each eyelet mark, and set an
   eyelet.
4. Measure A to B. **Flexed** and **Day 7** work as on T0.

## Via table

- Day 0
- Flexed
- Day 7

## 3 · Edge wraps

1. Lay 12 mm tape along the top edge so half overhangs, fold it onto the back down to the dashed
   line, and burnish it.
2. **One sheet:** measure from the front wrap to the back wrap.
3. **Side join:** overlap two sheets' top corners by 10 mm, wrap on wrap. Measure from one to the
   other, with and without a binder clip.
4. **Stack:** stack three sheets, top edges level, and clip along the edge. Measure from the top
   sheet's front wrap to the bottom sheet's back wrap, then again while turning the pages.

## Edge wrap table

- One sheet, front to back
- Side join, clipped
- Side join, no clip
- Stack, clipped
- Stack, turning pages

## 4 · Stacking shorts

1. On two sheets, lay the front pad and the S1 lane, the back pad on the back, and the wire via.
2. Lay sheet A on sheet B, shifted 15 mm to the left so B's S2 shows. A's back pad now lies on B's
   front pad.
3. Measure from A's S1 to B's S2, as listed below.

## Stacking table

- No interleaf
- Pressing hard
- Plain paper between
- Vellum between

## Pass or fail

- **Vias and wraps:** within 1 Ω of V0 and steady; flicker or OL fails.
- **Shorts:** any reading without an interleaf means stacked two-sided pages need one. With an
  interleaf it must read OL.
