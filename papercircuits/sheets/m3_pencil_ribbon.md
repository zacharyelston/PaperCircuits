# M3  Pencil ribbon

Slide a wiper along a pencil line, and the note glides.

## You need

- {R1.refs}: {R1}
- A soft 6B pencil and an eraser
- Card for the wiper, and clear tape
- Bare wire for {loops} patch loop
- A clip lead to M1's PITCH
- Copper tape, solder, 2-core cable

## Caption

Patch PITCH to M1's PITCH, then slide the wiper: right is high, left is low. GND passes through.

## Build it

1. Shade the ribbon hard with the 6B pencil, at least 10 times, until it shines. Rub out any smudge
   between it and the PITCH lane.
2. Lay the power ring and the PITCH lane, one piece of tape each.
3. Lay R1's bottom lead flat across the right end of the ribbon and hold it down with clear tape. {T1}
4. Solder R1's top lead to the V+ rail, or hold it flat with a small patch of copper tape. {T0}
5. Solder a patch loop upright on the PITCH loop mark. {T0}
6. {wiper_step}
7. {edge_join}

## Play it

- Patch PITCH to M1's PITCH. Press the wiper across the PITCH lane and the ribbon, and slide it.
- Find C4 to C5 with a tuner app and mark them in pen on the fret strip. If the note fades out on
  the left, shade that end darker.

## Labels

- frets: Fret strip: mark the notes here in pen
- low: low
- high: high
- wiper_strip: wiper strip
- pitch: PITCH on M1
- wiper: wiper

## Schematic note

The wiper picks how much ribbon is in the way.
