# L07  Share the voltage

Two resistors in a row split the supply between them. Find the rule.

## You need

- {R1.count} resistors, {R1} ({R1.bands})
- {R3.count} resistors, {R3} ({R3.bands})
- 5 mm copper tape
- Power page P1 in the binder
- A multimeter

## Build it

1. {lanes_step}
2. {flap_step}
3. {hole_step}
4. Sort the resistors by colour: four 1 kΩ and two 2.2 kΩ. Place each one on its labelled gap,
   either way round.
5. {hold_step}
6. Bind this page with P1. Hold the corner down while you measure.

## Labels

- switch: fold the corner down
- A: A · {R1} over {R2}
- B: B · {R3} over {R4}
- C: C · {R5} over {R6}

## Predict

- prompt: Which middle point will read the highest voltage?
- highest: Highest
- choices: A  /  B  /  C
- question: Circle one. Which resistor do you think gets the bigger share, and why?

## Measure

- prompt: Hold the corner down. Black probe on TP5.
- table: Middle
- A: A  TP2
- B: B  TP3
- C: C  TP4
- measured: Measured
- worked_out: Worked out
- rule: Worked out =
- rule_top: TP1 × bottom
- rule_bottom: ÷ (top + bottom)

## Explain

- prompt: What happens to the middle when you swap top and bottom?
- question: Compare B and C. Which pair would give you about 3 V from P1?

## Schematic note

A is R1 and R2, B is R3 and R4, C is R5 and R6.
