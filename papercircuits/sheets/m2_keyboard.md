# M2  Keyboard

Touch a key with the stylus lead from M1 to play a note.

## You need

- {R1.refs} {R1} · {R2.refs} {R2} · {R3.refs} {R3}
- {R4.refs} {R4} · {R5.refs} {R5} · {R6.refs} {R6}
- {R7.refs} {R7} · {R8.refs} {R8}
- A clip lead from M1's PITCH: the stylus
- Copper tape and solder
- 2-core cable to join the pages

## Caption

Touch anywhere on a key. Low C on the left, high C on the right. Only V+ is used; GND passes through.

## Build it

1. Lay the power ring and the {lanes} key lanes, one piece of tape each.
2. Place R1 from the top rail to the high C on the right. Place R2 to R8 between the keys,
   right to left, as labelled. Either way round.
3. {lead_step}
4. {edge_join}

## Play it

- Clip one end of a lead to M1's PITCH loop. Hold the other clip by its cover and touch its jaws to a key.
- Check each note with a tuner app on a phone. A note that's off can be tuned by swapping the
  resistor to its right for the next value up or down.

## Labels

- pitch: PITCH on M1
- stylus: stylus

## Schematic note

Each key further left adds a resistor: a lower note.
