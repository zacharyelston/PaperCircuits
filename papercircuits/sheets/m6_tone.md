# M6  Tone

A pencil line and a capacitor soften the sound.

## You need

- {C1.refs}: {C1}, no polarity (ceramic or film)
- A soft 6B pencil and an eraser
- Card for the wiper, and clear tape
- Bare wire for {loops} patch loops and a join
- Copper tape and solder
- 2-core cable to join the pages

## Caption

Patch M1's OUT to IN, and OUT to an IN on M7. Slide the wiper left for a softer sound.

## Build it

1. Shade the track hard with the 6B pencil, at least 10 times, until it shines. Rub out any smudge
   between it and the IN lane.
2. Lay the power ring, the IN lane and the OUT lane, one piece of tape each. Fold at the crease.
3. Lay a short bare wire from the OUT lane onto the track's right end, and hold it down with clear
   tape. {T1}
4. Place C1 from the OUT lane to the GND rail, either way round.
5. Solder C1's leads to their lanes, or hold them flat with a small patch of copper tape. {T0}
6. Solder a patch loop upright on the IN and OUT loop marks. {T0}
7. {wiper_step}
8. {edge_join}

## Play it

- Patch M1's OUT to IN, and OUT to M7's IN1. Press the wiper across the IN lane and the track.
- Near OUT the note buzzes. Slide left and it goes soft and round, and a little quieter.
