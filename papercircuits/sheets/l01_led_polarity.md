# L01  LEDs only work one way

Close the loop, then find out why an LED cares which way round it goes.

## You need

- 2 red LEDs, 5 mm
- 2 resistors, {R1} ({R1.bands}) {P}
- 5 mm copper tape
- Power page P1 in the binder
- A multimeter for Measure

## Build it

1. Lay the {lanes} lanes, one piece of tape each. Fold at the creases; never cut.
2. Stick a {flap_pad} mm piece of copper tape on the corner flap, S1. {T0}
3. {hole_step}
4. Place R1 and R2 across their gaps. Either way round works.
5. Place LED1 and LED2 **exactly as printed**: long leg on the +.
6. Hold each lead flat on its lane with a small patch of copper tape. {T0}
7. Bind this page with P1, then fold the corner down to switch on.

## Labels

- switch: fold the corner down

## Predict

- prompt: LED2 is printed the other way round.
- question: When you fold the corner down, which LEDs will light? Circle one for each.
- choices: lights  /  stays dark
- why: Why do you think so?

## Measure

- prompt: Hold the corner down. Black probe on TP4, red on each point.
- lit: Lit?
- yes_no: Y / N

## Explain

- prompt: Why does only one LED light?
- question: What would you change to make the other one light?

## Schematic note

V+ and GND come from P1 through the binder.
