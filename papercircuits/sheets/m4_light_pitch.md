# M4  Light pitch

Wave a hand over a light sensor, and the note follows.

## You need

- {LDR1.refs}: {LDR1}
- {R1.refs} {R1} · {R2.refs} {R2}
- Bare wire for {loops} patch loop
- A clip lead to M1's PITCH
- A desk lamp, for the widest range
- Copper tape, solder, 2-core cable

## Caption

Patch PITCH to M1's PITCH. Shade LDR1 for a lower note. Only V+ is used; GND passes through.

## Build it

1. Lay the power ring and the {lanes} lanes inside it, one piece of tape each.
2. Bend LDR1's legs flat in opposite directions, so its face looks up, and place it across the two
   lanes. Either way round.
3. Place R1 beside it, and R2 from the top rail to the upper lane. Either way round.
4. {lead_step}
5. Solder a patch loop upright on the PITCH loop mark. {T0}
6. {edge_join}

## Play it

- Patch PITCH to M1's PITCH. Hold a hand over LDR1 and lower it: the darker, the lower the note.
- Cut out the light tube, roll it round a pencil, glue the flap and slip it over LDR1. Now point it
  at a lamp to play.
- Take R1 out, and a full shadow fades the note to silence.

## Labels

- hand: Hold your hand over LDR1
- glue: glue
- tube: Light tube
- tube_steps: cut out, roll up and slip over LDR1
- ldr: light

## Schematic note

Less light, more resistance: a lower note on M1.
