# P1  Power page

3 × AA through a resettable fuse into V+ (H2) and GND (H3). Build it first.

## PDF title

P1 power page

## You need

- {BT1.refs}: 3 × AA holder with an on/off switch and wire leads {P}
- 3 AA alkaline cells {P}
- {F1.refs}: resettable fuse (PTC), {F1} {P}
- {R1.refs}: {R1} ({R1.bands}) {P}
- {LED1.refs}: {LED1} LED, 5 mm
- 5 mm copper tape, double-sided foam tape

## Build it

1. {lanes_step}
2. {hole_step}
3. Stick BT1 down with foam tape, wires toward the spine.
4. Strip 8 mm from each wire and twist it. Lay red along BAT+ and black along GND. {T0}
5. Place F1 and R1 across their gaps, either way round. Place LED1 as printed: long leg on the +.
6. Hold every lead and wire end flat with a patch of copper tape. {T0}
7. Fit the cells, switch BT1 on and fill in Check. Then switch it off.

## Labels

- plus: red
- minus: black

## Where P1 goes

BT1 is about 16 mm thick, so P1 goes last in the binder with the holder facing the back cover. Check
the rings or posts still close over it. {T0}

Switch BT1 off after each lesson. LED1 is lit whenever the book is on, as a reminder.

## Check

- prompt: Switch BT1 on. Black probe on TP3.
- lit: LED1 lit?
- yes_no: Y / N
- note: Fresh cells read about 4.5–4.8 V. TP1 and TP2 should match.

## Explain

- prompt: F1 is a fuse that resets itself.
- question: What could happen to the book if something shorted and F1 wasn't there?

## Before you bind it

Tick each one.

- BT1 is switched off.
- No bare wire end touches the other lane.
- P1 is the last page in the binder.
