# P1 · Power page — teacher notes

Page: [out/P1-power.pdf](../../out/P1-power.pdf). Status: draft. Build P1 before any lesson page; it goes last in the binder.

## What it does

- **BT1:** a 3 × AA holder with a built-in on/off switch. It is the book's master switch.
- **F1:** a resettable fuse between BT1's positive lead and the V+ hole (H2). BT1's negative lead goes straight to the GND hole (H3).
- **LED1 and R1:** sit after the fuse and light whenever the book is live. That tells learners three things: the bus has power, the fuse hasn't tripped, and they still need to switch off.

Each lesson page also has its own fold switch, so only the page being pressed draws current.

## Expected readings

Black probe on TP3 (GND), BT1 switched on:

| Point | Reading | Why |
|---|---|---|
| TP1 | 4.5–4.8 V fresh, lower as cells run down | The battery, before the fuse |
| TP2 | The same as TP1, within about 0.05 V | A cold PTC is a few ohms, so it drops almost nothing at these currents |

**LED1 current:** about (4.5 − 2.1) / 1000 ≈ 2.4 mA. That is enough to see and costs little battery: about 800 hours on 2000 mAh cells. It is still worth switching off.

## How the fuse behaves

- **When it trips:** if anything in the book shorts V+ to GND, the current jumps well past F1's 0.2 A trip level. F1 heats up within a second or two and its resistance rises sharply, cutting the current to a trickle. LED1 goes out and TP2 drops toward 0 V.
- **Resetting:** switch BT1 off, fix the short and wait a minute for F1 to cool. It resets by itself.
- **Without it:** 3 × AA can push several amps into a short. That heats the copper tape and the cells, and can lift tape or scorch paper. This is the answer the Explain box is after.

## Common faults

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| LED1 dark, TP1 ≈ 0 V | BT1 switched off, cells in backwards, or a wire end not making contact |
| LED1 dark, TP1 fine, TP2 ≈ 0 V | F1's leads aren't making contact, or F1 has tripped (it feels warm) |
| LED1 dark, TP2 fine | LED1 in backwards, or R1 or LED1 leads loose |
| LED1 dims as lessons are added | Cells running down, or too many pages pressed at once |

## Provisional choices

**[T0], waiting on the contact tests:**
- **Hole treatment** (step 2): eyelets in H2 and H3, pending T0 test 3.
- **Wire and lead attachment** (steps 4 and 6): a copper tape patch over twisted, stripped wire. Stranded wire is harder to hold than a solid part lead, so T0 test 2 may push this toward solder.
- **Binder capacity:** the holder's thickness has to fit whichever binding T0 picks.

**[P], waiting on the power decision:**
- **Supply:** 3 × AA at 4.5 V. If the book moves to 3 V, every lesson resistor changes. For example, L01 would use 100 Ω instead of 220 Ω.
- **Fuse:** 0.1 A hold, sized for a few pages on at once.
- **R1:** 1 kΩ, sized for 4.5 V.

To change these, edit the constants at the top of [`papercircuits/sheets/p1_power.py`](../../papercircuits/sheets/p1_power.py) and run `uv run build.py`.
