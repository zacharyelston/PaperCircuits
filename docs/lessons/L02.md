# L02 · Two switches, two ways — teacher notes

Page: [out/L02-and-or.pdf](../../out/L02-and-or.pdf). Status: draft. Needs [P1](P1.md) in the binder.

## The idea

There are two identical circuits, each a pair of switches, a resistor and an LED. The only difference is how the switches are wired:

- **Top, AND:** A1 and B1 are in a row (series). The current has to get through both, so LED1 lights only when both flaps are pressed. It's like a door with two locks.
- **Bottom, OR:** A2 and B2 sit side by side (parallel). Either flap gives the current a way through, so LED2 lights when either one is pressed. It's like a room with two doors.

This is the first step toward logic. It also previews series and parallel, which come back with LEDs and resistors in L05.

## The tab switches

Each switch is a flap cut from the page, as the standard's §6.2 describes:

- **Cutting:** learners cut the solid line, crease the dotted hinge, and stick a 10 mm piece of tape on the flap.
- **Closing:** pressed down, the flap's tape lands across two lanes that run 2 mm apart and joins them.
- **Momentary:** the card springs back, so the switch is on only while it's held.

## Expected results

| Pressed | LED1 (top) | TP1 | LED2 (bottom) | TP2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Only A | dark | ≈ 0 V | lit | ≈ 4.5 V |
| Only B | dark | ≈ 0 V | lit | ≈ 4.5 V |
| Both | lit | ≈ 4.5 V | lit | ≈ 4.5 V |

Both readings use TP3 (GND) as the reference. TP1 and TP2 sit before the resistors, so they read the full supply whenever their switches connect them to V+. When the switches are open, the point is cut off from V+ and reads about 0 V.

## Common faults

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| An LED lights with no flap pressed | The 2 mm gap in a switch pair is bridged: tape edges touching, or a crumb of tape across the gap |
| A flap does nothing | Its tape doesn't cross both lanes when folded, or the crease holds it too far open. Press firmly. |
| An LED is dim or flickers while pressed | Light flap pressure; the contact is copper pressed on copper |
| LED1 lights with only B1 pressed | V+ reaches the middle lane: check that the V+ lane stops short of B1 |
