# L07 · Share the voltage — teacher notes

Page: [out/L07-dividers.pdf](../../out/L07-dividers.pdf). Status: draft. Needs [P1](P1.md) in the binder.

## The idea

Two resistors in a row share the supply between them, in proportion to their size. The point between them, the middle, sits at:

> middle = supply × bottom ÷ (top + bottom)

The page has three dividers:
- **A:** 1 kΩ over 1 kΩ, so half the supply.
- **B:** 2.2 kΩ over 1 kΩ, so about a third.
- **C:** 1 kΩ over 2.2 kΩ, so about two thirds.

B and C use the same two resistors, swapped, so learners see that the bigger resistor takes the bigger share. There are no LEDs on this page; the meter does the showing. It sets up sensors later on, where one of the two resistors changes with light or touch.

## Expected readings

With P1 at 4.5 V, black probe on TP5, corner held down. If TP1 reads differently, everything scales with it.

| Divider | Middle | Worked out |
|---|---|---|
| A, TP2 | about 2.25 V | 4.5 × 1 ÷ 2 |
| B, TP3 | about 1.4 V | 4.5 × 1 ÷ 3.2 |
| C, TP4 | about 3.1 V | 4.5 × 2.2 ÷ 3.2 |

With ±5 % resistors, expect readings within about 0.1 V of the worked-out values.

**Explain answers:**
- Swapping top and bottom flips the share: B against C.
- C gives about 3 V from 4.5 V.

## Common faults

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| A middle reads the same as TP1 | The bottom resistor isn't connecting |
| A middle reads 0 V | The top resistor isn't connecting, or the corner isn't held down |
| B and C read the same | The 1 kΩ and 2.2 kΩ were placed the same way round in both. Re-read the colours. |
