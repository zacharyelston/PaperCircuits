# L08 · Store some charge — teacher notes

Page: [out/L08-capacitor.pdf](../../out/L08-capacitor.pdf). Status: draft. Needs [P1](P1.md) in the binder.

## The idea

A capacitor stores electric charge and gives it back, like a tiny, fast battery.

- **While the corner is held,** C1 (2200 µF) charges through R1.
- **When it's released,** the supply is gone, but C1 keeps pushing current through R2 and LED1. LED1 fades out over a few seconds.
- **LED2,** with just a resistor, goes out the instant the corner is released. The side-by-side comparison makes the effect obvious.

## Expected results

With P1 at 4.5 V and red LEDs:

| Press | LED1 glows for | LED2 glows for |
|---|---|---|
| A quick tap | a second or two | 0 s |
| Hold 1 s | about 2–4 s | 0 s |
| Hold 5 s | the same as 1 s | 0 s |

Even a 1 s hold fills C1. It charges through R1 in about R1 × C1 ≈ 0.2 s, so holding longer changes nothing. That's a nice discovery for learners.

The fade time is set by R2 × C1, about 1 s. LED1 dims smoothly and stops glowing once TP2 falls to about 1.6–1.8 V: an LED needs about that much to light at all. That's the "TP2 when LED1 goes dark" reading.

**Making it last longer:** a bigger capacitor, or a bigger R2. A bigger R2 also makes LED1 dimmer.

## Safety

- **Polarity matters.** An electrolytic capacitor connected backwards can heat up, bulge, and in the worst case vent. The page marks the − stripe, and the steps say to check it. Use a capacitor rated 10 V or more; the supply is 4.5 V.
- **Pressing the corner** draws a brief surge of about 45 mA through R1 into the empty capacitor. That's well within R1's rating and P1's fuse.
- **After use** C1 keeps a little charge, about 1.7 V. That's harmless, but it's why TP2 doesn't read 0 V afterwards.

## Common faults

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| LED1 goes out as fast as LED2 | C1's + leg isn't touching node X, or C1 is backwards |
| LED1 doesn't light at all | R2 or LED1 not connecting, or LED1 backwards |
| C1 feels warm | C1 is backwards. Switch BT1 off at once and turn it round. |

## Provisional choices

- **[T0]:** corner switch pad, hole treatment and lead attachment, as in L01.
- **[P] R1, R2, R3:** chosen for 4.5 V. At 3 V the fade is shorter and dimmer.
