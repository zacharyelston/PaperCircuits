# L01 · LEDs only work one way — teacher notes

Page: [out/L01-led-polarity.pdf](../../out/L01-led-polarity.pdf). Status: draft.

## The idea

An LED lets current through in one direction only: into the long leg (anode, +) and out of the short leg (cathode). The page has two identical branches, each a resistor and an LED, both on the same switched supply. LED2 is printed reversed on purpose. When learners place both LEDs exactly as printed, only LED1 lights.

Folding the corner down (S1) closes the loop. That's the second lesson on the page: nothing lights until the circuit is a complete loop.

## Flow

1. **Predict** before building: which LEDs will light?
2. **Build:** five lanes, the flap pad, two resistors, two LEDs.
3. **Switch on and observe.**
4. **Measure** TP1–TP3 against TP4.
5. **Explain** why only one LED lit, and what would make the other light: turn LED2 round.

## Expected readings

With P1 at 4.5 V (3 × AA) and red LEDs:

| Point | Reading | Why |
|---|---|---|
| TP1 | ≈ 4.5 V | The supply, after the switch |
| TP2 | ≈ 1.8–2.2 V | LED1's forward voltage; R1 drops the rest |
| TP3 | ≈ 4.5 V, same as TP1 | No current flows in branch 2, so R2 drops nothing and the whole supply sits across LED2 |

TP3 is the clue for older learners. A dark LED isn't missing voltage; it's blocking current.

**Current:** LED1 draws about (4.5 − 2.0) / 220 ≈ 11 mA. LED2 draws none. That is within the standard's 20 mA page budget.

## Common faults

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Neither LED lights | Flap pad missing or not meeting both lanes; an overlapped piece of tape in a lane; P1 not bound or its batteries are flat |
| Both LEDs light | LED2 was placed the "right" way instead of as printed. Check that TP3 reads about 2 V. |
| TP2 reads the same as TP1 | LED1's leads aren't making contact |
| LED1 flickers when pressed | A loose lead contact. This is what T0 test 2 measures. |

## Provisional choices

- **[T0] Hole treatment** (step 3): eyelets in H2 and H3, pending T0 test 3.
- **[T0] Lead attachment** (step 6): a copper tape patch over flat leads, pending T0 test 2.
- **[P] Supply:** 220 Ω assumes [P1](P1.md) gives 4.5 V. With 3 V (2 × AA or a coin cell), use 100 Ω.
- **LED2 reverse voltage:** LED2 sits reversed across the full supply. Most 5 mm LEDs are rated for 5 V reverse, so keep P1 below 5 V while this page is in the binder.
